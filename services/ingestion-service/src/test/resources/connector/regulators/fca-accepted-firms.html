Regulatory Sandbox accepted firms

First published: 27/03/2022 Last updated: 24/07/2026 See all updates

Our Regulatory Sandbox is for firms to test new products live in the market with real consumers. This page lists the firms we have accepted.

  • Stablecoin cohort
  • List of accepted firms

If a firm is listed on this page, it is carrying out testing as part of our Regulatory Sandbox, or has done so in the past.

As part of the Sandbox, we may give firms temporary permissions, if appropriate, to carry out certain activities. We'll update our Financial Services Register to reflect this.

Always check the firm or person you're dealing with is listed on the Register.

Check the Register

Firms not listed on the FCA might include, for example, RegTech or SupTech providers which provide technology solutions and are not necessarily carrying out regulated activity. 

Firms can apply to our Regulatory Sandbox throughout the year. Before 2021, we used a cohort model.

Note: we decide which firms and propositions we accept into the Regulatory Sandbox on a case-by-case basis, depending whether applicants meet our eligibility criteria.

Being accepted to the Sandbox previously does not mean you will be accepted in the future.

Find how to apply and test in the Regulatory Sandbox.
